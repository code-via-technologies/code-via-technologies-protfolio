Privacy Policy for Gilded Pyramid Solitaire

Effective November 2026

Quick summary: Gilded Pyramid Solitaire has no accounts and no server of its own. Your games and statistics stay on your device. The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device and advertising data to deliver those ads. You choose whether ads may be personalised, and a one-time purchase removes ads entirely.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. We do not run a server for Gilded Pyramid Solitaire and we do not receive any information about you or how you play. The app has no sign-in, no analytics SDK of our own, and no crash reporting SDK.

What stays on your device

Your statistics (games played, wins, best time, daily streak) and your settings are saved only on your device. They are not synced or sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them, and you can reset them at any time in the app's Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gilded Pyramid Solitaire shows banner ads, full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded ads that unlock a hint. These are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and limit how often you see the same ad, Google's advertising SDK inside the app may collect:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, and app version;
  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • how you interact with ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic and performance data about ad delivery.

This data goes directly from your device to Google and its advertising partners. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Google explains how it uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and its privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • Tracking permission. iOS asks whether Gilded Pyramid Solitaire may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. If you are in one of these regions, the app shows a consent message before any ads are requested. You can accept or decline personalised ads, and you can change your choice later from Privacy options in the app's Settings.
  • Remove ads. The one-time Remove ads purchase turns off all ads. After it, the app no longer requests ads from Google.

Purchases

Remove ads is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information.

Children's privacy

Gilded Pyramid Solitaire is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this privacy policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before that change ships, with the effective date revised.
