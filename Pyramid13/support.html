Pyramid 13 Support

How to play

  • Tap two uncovered cards that add up to 13 to remove them. Aces count 1, Jacks 11, Queens 12.
  • Kings count 13 and are removed with a single tap.
  • A card is uncovered when no card overlaps it from the row below.
  • Tap the stock to turn a card onto the waste pile. The top waste card can be paired with an uncovered pyramid card.
  • When the stock runs out you can turn the waste pile over and go through it again, twice per game.
  • Clear the whole pyramid to win.

Daily deal

Everyone gets the same deal on the same day, and every daily deal can be won. Winning on consecutive days builds your streak. Missing a day resets it.

Remove ads and restoring your purchase

Remove ads is a one-time purchase. If you reinstall the app or change phones, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Ad privacy choices

You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
