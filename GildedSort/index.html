Gilded Sort

A water-sort puzzle for iPhone. Pour colors into matching bottles and solve each puzzle at your own pace.

How to play

Numbered levels

Play without a timer. Undo a pour, restart for free, or come back later to the same board.

A daily puzzle for everyone

Play the same new puzzle as everyone else each UTC day. Finish on consecutive dates to build a streak.

Hints when you want them

An optional video can reveal a move toward the solution. Hints are free with Remove Ads or when a video is unavailable.
