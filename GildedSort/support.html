Gilded Sort Support

How to play

  1. Tap a bottle with liquid, then tap an empty bottle or one with the same color on top and room for another layer.
  2. Each pour moves as many connected layers of the top color as will fit. A bottle holds four layers.
  3. Finish with each bottle either empty or full of one color.

Puzzles have no time limit or pour limit. Undo and restart are free. Turn on Color symbols in Settings to distinguish colors by symbol. VoiceOver describes bottle contents from bottom to top.

Pour targets and stars

Every puzzle has a three-star pour target based on a verified solution. Finish within that target for three stars. Finish within the two-star threshold shown in the game for two stars. Every other completion earns one star.

Each successful pour counts. Undo restores the bottles and keeps the pour count. Restart gives you the same puzzle with a fresh count. Your best star rating for each numbered level and daily puzzle is saved on this device.

Daily puzzle and streaks

Everyone gets the same puzzle for the current UTC date. A new one arrives at 00:00 UTC, which may be at a different time from your local midnight. An open daily puzzle keeps the date it started with.

A daily streak counts completed puzzles on consecutive UTC dates, once per date. You can see your streak and today’s best pour count in Statistics.

Video hints

A hint shows one move on a verified path to the solution. If a video is offered, watch it through to earn the hint. Hints are free with Remove Ads or when a video is unavailable.

Remove Ads and restore purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that removes banner and between-puzzle ads and gives you hints without a video. To restore it after reinstalling or changing phones, sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy it. Open Gilded Sort’s Settings and choose Restore purchases.

If a purchase is pending, wait for Apple’s confirmation. Contact us if a completed purchase still does not appear after you restore it. For billing or refund questions, use Apple’s purchase support.

Saved progress

Numbered-level progress, daily results, settings, and the unfinished puzzle are stored on the device. Removing the app can remove your progress. Restore purchases restores the Remove Ads purchase.

Privacy settings

Turn off Share usage analytics in Settings to stop collection and request deletion of prior usage data from this installation. Choose Delete usage data to request deletion and keep your sharing preference. A pending request retries when the app can connect.

Use Advertising privacy choices in the game’s Settings when available. Manage Apple’s tracking permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Gilded Sort privacy policy.
