What Slimreel Does

Slimreel shrinks video files entirely on your device. Pick a target — WhatsApp, email, or a size cap — or a quality tier, and Slimreel estimates the output size before you commit, so you're never guessing. Nothing is ever uploaded to a server: all compression happens locally using on-device video processing.

How to Use Slimreel

  1. Tap Pick a Video and choose a video from your Photos library
  2. Choose a target size (WhatsApp, email, 100 MB) or a quality tier (High, Medium, Small)
  3. Check the live estimated output size shown for your chosen preset
  4. Tap Compress and wait for Slimreel to finish, entirely on-device
  5. Save the result as a new video or replace the original, or share it directly from the result screen

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why is my output still large?

A: The Original quality tier is a passthrough tier — it re-encodes at (close to) the source bitrate and resolution, so the output size will be close to the original. To shrink the file, pick a target size (WhatsApp, email, or a custom cap) or a lower quality tier such as Medium or Small instead.

Q: How many videos can I compress for free?

A: The free tier includes 3 compressions per day, with every quality and target-size preset available and no watermark. Slimreel Pro removes the daily limit and adds 4K output and batch compression, for a single one-time payment — never a subscription.

Q: How do I restore my Slimreel Pro purchase?

A: Open Slimreel, go to the Pro / upgrade screen, and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used to buy Slimreel Pro.

Q: Does Slimreel need an internet connection?

A: No. Slimreel works completely offline, including in airplane mode — it makes zero network calls of any kind.

Q: Can I compress more than one video at a time?

A: Yes, with Slimreel Pro. Select up to 20 videos at once and Slimreel will work through them one at a time, with a single summary when it's done.

Q: Is my video data private?

A: Yes. Slimreel has no accounts, no analytics, and no servers of its own. Videos are read from and written back to your device's Photos library using Apple's on-device tools and are never uploaded anywhere. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Troubleshooting

Compression is taking a long time

Compression time depends on the video's length, resolution, and your device's performance, since everything runs on-device. Longer or higher-resolution videos, and older devices, will take longer. Keep the app open and your device unlocked until the process finishes.

"Daily limit reached" message

The free tier allows 3 compressions per day. The limit resets daily, or you can upgrade to Slimreel Pro for unlimited compressions.
