Show or edit a card

In My passes, tap the round Show button or swipe all the way right to show a card. Tap its name for Card details. Swipe left or use the three-dot button for Edit design and Quick access.

Add a card to Apple Wallet

Open a saved card and tap Add to Apple Wallet. You need an internet connection and an iPhone that supports Apple Wallet. Confirm in the system sheet. If you cancel or the request fails, the card is still in My passes. To use an existing ticket or boarding pass, enter or scan the original issuer’s code.

Open a card from a widget or a sharing session

Add a Pass Studio widget to the Home Screen or Lock Screen and choose a card in the widget’s settings. To start a Live Activity, open the card’s Quick access screen and tap Start sharing session. On a compatible iPhone, you can also open it from the Dynamic Island.

Restore or manage your subscription

Pass Studio Monthly costs US $4.99 per month in the U.S. App Store. Apple shows the current local price before you subscribe. The subscription renews each month until canceled.

Use Restore purchases under Pass Studio’s Settings → Subscription with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. Use Manage subscription there, or open Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings to cancel. Saved cards remain available to show after the subscription ends.
