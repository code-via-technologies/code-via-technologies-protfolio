Wallet Press Support

Wallet Press (previously Pass Studio) is operated by Tom Weiss, trading as Code Via Technologies.

Email tom.weiss.t@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Wallet Press version, and the steps that led to the problem. If you attach a screenshot, cover any private details and scannable codes.

Show or edit a card

In My passes, tap the round Show button or swipe all the way right to show a card. Tap its name for Card details. Swipe left or use the three-dot button for Edit design and Quick access.

Add a card to Apple Wallet

Wallet Press signing is currently on hold. The planned service issues business and custom cards you create and have the rights to share. Event, membership and boarding designs stay in the app. For travel or admission, use the original issuer’s credential.

When signing is available, open the card and tap Add to Apple Wallet. Review the fields, code and artwork on your iPhone. Tap Send for signing to send those items and your subscription proof to the signing service, then confirm in Apple Wallet. Cancel during the review to keep the design local. Passes you have already installed are available directly in Apple Wallet.

Open a card from a widget or a sharing session

Add a Wallet Press widget to the Home Screen or Lock Screen and choose a card in the widget’s settings. To start a Live Activity, open the card’s Quick access screen and tap Start sharing session. On a compatible iPhone, you can also open it from the Dynamic Island.

Restore or manage your subscription

Wallet Press Monthly costs US $4.99 per month in the U.S. App Store. Apple shows the current local price before you subscribe. The subscription renews each month until canceled.

Use Restore purchases under Wallet Press’s Settings → Subscription with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. Use Manage subscription there, or open Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings to cancel. Saved cards remain available to show after the subscription ends.
