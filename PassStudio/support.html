Wallet Press Support

Wallet Press (previously Pass Studio) is operated by Tom Weiss, trading as Code Via Technologies.

Email tom.weiss.t@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Wallet Press version, and the steps that led to the problem. If you attach a screenshot, cover any private details and scannable codes.

Show or edit a card

In My passes, tap Show beneath the card or swipe all the way right to present it. Tap the card face for Card details. Swipe left or use the three-dot button for Edit design and Quick access.

Add a card to Apple Wallet

Wallet Press 1.0 build 3 lets you design and share all five card types locally. Adding or updating an Apple Wallet pass is unavailable in this build. If you already have a pass in Apple Wallet, open it there at any time, including after your subscription expires. Use the original issuer’s credential for admission, membership and travel.

Open a card from a widget or a sharing session

Add a Wallet Press widget to the Home Screen or Lock Screen and choose a card in the widget’s settings. To start a Live Activity, open the card’s Quick access screen and tap Start sharing session. On a compatible iPhone, you can also open it from the Dynamic Island.

Restore or manage your subscription

Wallet Press Monthly costs US $4.99 per month in the U.S. App Store. Apple shows the current local price before you subscribe. The subscription renews each month until canceled.

Use Restore purchases under Wallet Press’s Settings → Subscription with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. Use Manage subscription there, or open Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings to cancel. Saved cards remain available to show after the subscription ends.

To request a refund for an Apple purchase, sign in to Report a Problem and choose “Request a refund.” Eligibility depends on your circumstances and storefront. For a withdrawal request, billing dispute or help finding the right contact for your purchase, email us or follow Apple’s refund instructions. You retain any mandatory consumer rights in your country.

Sharing cards and reporting misuse

Only share details, codes and artwork you have permission to use. Get permission before sharing another person’s contact details, portrait or an employer’s logo. Do not impersonate an issuer or present a design as permission to travel, enter an event or redeem a benefit. Check the preview before sharing. Sharing uses the recipient or app you choose in iOS. Wallet Press has no public card directory.

In the app, open Settings → Help → Report a pass or privacy concern, or email Tom Weiss. Describe the issue, how you received the card and what you would like us to do. Mask private details and scannable codes. Tom reviews the report, requests additional information if needed, and replies about the action he can take. A card another person already received is under that recipient’s control.
