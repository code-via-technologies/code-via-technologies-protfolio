Wallet Press Privacy Policy

Updated 25 November 2026. Tom Weiss, trading as Code Via Technologies in Israel, operates Wallet Press (previously Pass Studio) and is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact tom.weiss.t@gmail.com.

Wallet Press does not require an account. The app contains no advertising or analytics tracking.

Your designs, camera, and photos

Wallet Press stores your saved cards, codes, notes, and original artwork in its local library. It does not sync that library to a Wallet Press account or server. Your device’s backup settings may apply to local app data.

If you choose to scan a code, Wallet Press requests camera access and reads the code on your iPhone. If you use Photos, the app receives the images you select. You can revoke camera access in iPhone Settings.

Adding to Apple Wallet

In Wallet Press 1.0 build 3, all five card types stay in the app unless you choose to share them. This build cannot send a design, artwork or purchase proof to a signing service. You can still open passes you already have in Apple Wallet.

Earlier Pass Studio builds could send contact, ticket, travel or membership details to the PassJar signer when you tapped Add or Update, before Wallet displayed its confirmation. These could include notes, extra fields, email and phone even when the contact QR omitted them. The signer processed the selected design’s fields, code and rendered artwork in memory to return a signed pass. Original photo files stayed on the device.

The existing PassJar signing service, used by earlier builds and explicitly enabled developer previews, runs on Railway. Railway records HTTP request metadata, including the source IP address, client user agent, request path, time, and response status, to operate and troubleshoot the service. Railway makes these logs available for seven days under our current hosting plan. Our signing application also keeps the request’s IP address and a request count in memory to limit abuse, with expired entries cleared on an hourly schedule.

When you add a signed pass, Apple Wallet manages its copy under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Widgets, Live Activities, and sharing

To let you choose a saved card, the app makes card summaries and available thumbnails accessible to its widget on this device. In the updated app, the picker lists card names and types. Unselected thumbnails require an unlocked device. The widget can keep the selected card’s thumbnail available while the phone is locked. A sharing session may also show the role and company on a business card. The title, photo, logo and company can be visible to someone looking at your Home Screen, Lock Screen, or Dynamic Island. You can choose another card, remove the widget, or end the sharing session. If you share a full image, the recipient can see everything in that image, including details outside a code. A recipient who scans a contact code receives its selected contact fields.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payment through the App Store. Wallet Press checks your verified subscription status on your device. The signing-disabled version checks Apple’s purchase information on the device to unlock paid features. We do not receive your payment card details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Delete your data or contact us

Use Delete card in Card details to remove a saved card. You can also delete Wallet Press to remove its local library from the device. Delete card also clears its stored Wallet update dates in build 3. Open the updated app to let it clean up records for previously deleted cards. Removing the app removes its library, but iOS can retain widget Keychain records across uninstall until the next first launch. End any active sharing session and remove its widget if you no longer want it displayed. Remove an installed Wallet pass separately in Wallet. Deleting a card cannot recall a copy sent to someone else or erase an earlier iPhone backup. Manage backups in your Apple Account or on the computer that made them.

Why we process data and your rights

We use the information you send to provide the export or assistance you request. We use limited technical records to protect the service and prevent misuse. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of the requested service for exports and our legitimate interests in responding to requests and keeping the service secure. Card design and local storage work without sending a pass to the signer. Only include other people’s details or images when you have permission to use them.

GitHub (public pages), Railway (earlier pass signing), Apple (purchases and Wallet) and Google (support mail) may process information outside your country. You can contact Tom to ask where your information is processed, about transfer safeguards, or to request access, correction or deletion. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict processing, object, obtain a portable copy or complain to your local data protection authority. To remove local copies from your device or Wallet, use the steps above.
