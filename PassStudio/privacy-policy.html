Pass Studio Privacy Policy

Pass Studio does not require an account. The app contains no advertising or analytics tracking.

Your designs, camera, and photos

Pass Studio stores your saved cards, codes, notes, and original artwork in its local library. It does not sync that library to a Pass Studio account or server. Your device’s backup settings may apply to local app data.

If you choose to scan a code, Pass Studio requests camera access and reads the code on your iPhone. If you use Photos, the app receives the images you select. You can revoke camera access in iPhone Settings.

Adding to Apple Wallet

When you choose to add or update a pass in Apple Wallet, Pass Studio sends that pass’s fields, code, and derived artwork over HTTPS to our signing service, hosted by Railway. This can include contact, ticket, travel, or membership details that you entered. Original photo files stay in your local library. Our signing application processes the pass contents in memory to sign and return the pass, then discards them.

Railway records HTTP request metadata, including the source IP address, client user agent, request path, time, and response status, to operate and troubleshoot the service. Railway makes these logs available for seven days under our current hosting plan. Our signing application also keeps the request’s IP address and a request count in memory to limit abuse, with expired entries cleared on an hourly schedule.

When you add a signed pass, Apple Wallet manages its copy under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Widgets, Live Activities, and sharing

The widget and Live Activity use a local summary and a small illustration of the selected card. Its title, photo, or logo can be visible on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, or Dynamic Island. You can choose another card, remove the widget, or end the sharing session. If you tap Share or show your code to someone, the recipient or app you choose can receive the information you share.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payment through the App Store. Pass Studio checks your verified subscription status on your device. We do not receive your payment card details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Delete your data or contact us

Use Delete card in Card details to remove a saved card. You can also delete Pass Studio to remove its local library from the device. Remove a pass added to Apple Wallet separately in Wallet.

Email tom.weiss.t@gmail.com with a privacy question or a request to access, correct, or delete personal information held in support correspondence. We use the email address and information you send to handle and follow up on your request.
