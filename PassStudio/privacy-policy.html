Wallet Press Privacy Policy

Updated 25 November 2026. Tom Weiss, trading as Code Via Technologies in Israel, operates Wallet Press (previously Pass Studio) and is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact tom.weiss.t@gmail.com.

Wallet Press does not require an account. The app contains no advertising or analytics tracking.

Your designs, camera, and photos

Wallet Press stores your saved cards, codes, notes, and original artwork in its local library. It does not sync that library to a Wallet Press account or server. Your device’s backup settings may apply to local app data.

If you choose to scan a code, Wallet Press requests camera access and reads the code on your iPhone. If you use Photos, the app receives the images you select. You can revoke camera access in iPhone Settings.

Adding to Apple Wallet

Exporting is optional. Wallet signing sends pass fields, codes and derived artwork over HTTPS to our signing service. In versions with an export review, you choose the optional details and see what will be sent before confirming. Earlier Pass Studio builds start signing when you tap Add or Update. They can include notes, extra fields, email and phone even when the contact QR omits those details. The Apple Wallet confirmation appears after signing. Earlier builds could send contact, ticket, travel or membership details that you entered. The dedicated Wallet Press service is intended for your own business and custom cards. Event, membership and boarding designs stay in the app when using this version. Original photo files stay in your local library. Our signing application processes the pass contents in memory to sign and return the pass, then discards them.

The existing PassJar signing service, used by earlier builds and the iPhone preview, runs on Railway. Railway records HTTP request metadata, including the source IP address, client user agent, request path, time, and response status, to operate and troubleshoot the service. Railway makes these logs available for seven days under our current hosting plan. Our signing application also keeps the request’s IP address and a request count in memory to limit abuse, with expired entries cleared on an hourly schedule.

When you add a signed pass, Apple Wallet manages its copy under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Widgets, Live Activities, and sharing

To let you choose a saved card, the app makes card summaries and available thumbnails accessible to its widget on this device. In the updated app, the picker lists card names and types. Unselected thumbnails require an unlocked device. The widget can keep the selected card’s thumbnail available while the phone is locked. A sharing session may also show the role and company on a business card. The title, photo, logo and company can be visible to someone looking at your Home Screen, Lock Screen, or Dynamic Island. You can choose another card, remove the widget, or end the sharing session. If you share a full image, the recipient can see everything in that image, including details outside a code. A recipient who scans a contact code receives its selected contact fields.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payment through the App Store. Wallet Press checks your verified subscription status on your device. When the dedicated Wallet Press signing service is used, it also receives Apple-signed transaction evidence and, when needed, renewal evidence to verify your subscription. It uses the original transaction identifier to limit signing requests for the subscription within an hourly window and to generate a pass serial so that you can update the same card. The pass does not contain your Apple transaction identifier. Rate-limit entries expire after the window and are cleared when new requests are processed or the service restarts. We do not receive your payment card details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Delete your data or contact us

Use Delete card in Card details to remove a saved card. You can also delete Wallet Press to remove its local library from the device. Remove a pass added to Apple Wallet separately in Wallet.

Why we process data and your rights

We use the information you send to provide the export or assistance you request. We use limited technical records to protect the service and prevent misuse. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of the requested service for exports and our legitimate interests in responding to requests and keeping the service secure. Card design and local storage work without sending a pass to the signer. Only include other people’s details or images when you have permission to use them.

GitHub, Railway, Apple and our email provider may process information outside your country. You can contact Tom to ask where your information is processed, about transfer safeguards, or to request access, correction or deletion. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict processing, object, obtain a portable copy or complain to your local data protection authority.
