Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

Quick Summary: Digital Wallet stores your voucher and card data locally on your device. When you generate a pass, its contents are sent to a server we operate so it can be cryptographically signed for Apple Wallet, and are deleted from that server immediately afterward — see Section 2 and Section 5. Separately, the app sends anonymous, on-by-default usage analytics to a server we operate; see Section 3. Neither is shared with third parties or used for advertising.

1. Overview

Digital Wallet ("the App") is an iOS application that helps you create and manage digital passes for Apple Wallet. Your privacy is extremely important to us, which is why we've designed the app to keep your voucher and card data on your device, and to send to our server only what is strictly necessary to sign a pass or to record anonymous usage analytics.

2. Information We Collect

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Voucher details (card names, balances, expiration dates)
  • Card numbers you enter
  • Additional notes you add to cards
  • App preferences (language, appearance settings)
  • Custom card templates you create

When you generate a pass for Apple Wallet, the pass contents you've entered (the fields above) are sent to a signing server we operate, because .pkpass files must be cryptographically signed with our Apple Wallet certificate — a step that cannot happen on your device without shipping that certificate inside the app. The server signs the pass, returns it to the app, and deletes the uploaded contents immediately afterward. See Section 5 for details.

Separately, the app sends anonymous usage analytics to a different server we operate. See Section 3 for the complete list of what that includes.

3. Usage Analytics

Digital Wallet sends anonymous, on-by-default usage analytics to a server we operate. It is a short list of events, and nothing else:

  • app_open — with a flag for whether it was a cold start
  • first_launch
  • purchase — product id, transaction id, and whether it was a purchase or a restore
  • paywall_view — where the paywall was shown from
  • feature_used — the name of a feature, from a fixed list we define
  • error — an error domain and a numeric code
  • opt_out — recorded when you turn analytics off

Alongside each event we record a random ID generated on your device when you first install the app — not an Apple device identifier, not shared with any other app, and not linked to your identity — plus app version, iOS version, device model (e.g. "iPhone16,2"), language, and your 2-letter country. There are no free-text fields. Analytics never sees your voucher details, card numbers, notes, or anything else you've typed.

We do not store IP addresses, even in logs; an IP is hashed with a key that rotates daily, used only to rate-limit abuse, and then discarded. Raw events are deleted after 90 days. Analytics never goes to an ad network, an analytics SDK, or any other third party — it goes to a server we operate ourselves and nowhere else. Nothing is sold or shared.

You can turn analytics off at any time in the app's settings. Opting out stops new events from being sent and deletes the events already collected from your install.

4. How We Use Your Information

All information you enter is used exclusively to:

  • Display your saved vouchers and loyalty cards
  • Generate digital passes for Apple Wallet
  • Customize your app experience

5. Data Storage

  • Stored On Your Device: Your vouchers, loyalty cards and photos are stored using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults and the local file system)
  • No Cloud Sync: Your saved cards are never synchronized to cloud services
  • The Pass Signing Server: Apple requires every Wallet pass to be cryptographically signed, and signing has to happen on a server — putting the signing certificate inside the app would expose it. So at the moment you generate a pass, that pass's contents (its name, colours, logo and barcode value) are sent over an encrypted connection to a signing server we operate. The server signs the pass, sends it straight back, and deletes the uploaded file and its working directory immediately, on both success and failure. It is not written to a database and its contents are not logged. Nothing else in the app is sent there.
  • iOS Backup: Your data may be included in iOS device backups (iCloud or iTunes)

6. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell or share your data with third parties. Period.

  • No advertising networks
  • No third-party trackers
  • No data brokers
  • No third-party analytics SDKs — the usage statistics described in section 3 go only to our own server

7. Apple Wallet Integration

When you add a pass to Apple Wallet, the pass data is transmitted to Apple's Wallet app on your device. This is a standard iOS function and is necessary for the app to work. We do not control how Apple Wallet stores or manages passes.

8. Data Security

Your vouchers and cards are stored on your device, protected by your device's security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). We recommend keeping your device secure and using device encryption features provided by iOS.

9. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your saved vouchers within the app
  • Edit: Modify or update any voucher information
  • Delete: Remove individual vouchers or delete the entire app
  • Export: Generate passes to Apple Wallet at any time

10. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Parents may want to supervise young children's use of the app to ensure they understand how to use it appropriately.
