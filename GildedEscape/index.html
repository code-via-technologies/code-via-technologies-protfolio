Gilded Escape

Coming soon for iPhone. Slide the blocks to clear a path for the gold block and get it to the exit on the right.

How to play

64 levels to solve

Start with a few slides and work up to trickier boards. Match the move target for three stars, or take as many moves as you need.

A fresh Daily Escape

Come back for the puzzle of the day. Finish on consecutive dates to build a streak and share your result.

Hints when you’re stuck

A hint shows a slide toward the exit. An optional video can unlock the hint. Hints are free with Remove Ads or when a video is unavailable.
