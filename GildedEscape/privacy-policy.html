Gilded Escape Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Code VIA Technologies publishes Gilded Escape. The game saves your progress on your device and works without a game account. Google provides ads and advertising privacy choices. Apple processes purchases.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps your unfinished board, moves, undo history, level and daily progress, best ratings, and settings on your device. Removing the app can remove this progress. To delete usage data associated with your installation, use the in-app control before uninstalling.

Usage analytics

Usage analytics are on by default when the service is available. We use these data to understand how people use the game and where to improve it. Events include app opens, puzzle starts and outcomes, use of features such as daily play or hints, ad impressions and rewards, and views or verified transactions for Remove Ads.

Analytics records can contain a randomly generated installation identifier, event IDs and timestamps, app and iOS versions, device model, and language and country or region from device settings. A verified purchase event can include the product identifier, Apple transaction identifier, and whether it was a purchase or restore.

Railway hosts our analytics service. The service receives your IP address when connecting. It uses a hash with a daily rotating key to prevent abuse, and analytics records exclude raw IP addresses. Infrastructure providers may process connection or security logs to operate their services.

Analytics choices and deletion

Turn off Share Usage Analytics in the game’s Settings to stop collecting usage analytics and request deletion of existing usage data for this installation. If the app is offline, it keeps the request pending and retries when it can connect.

After server confirmation, the active analytics database removes installation-linked events, purchase telemetry, and the installation record. The service keeps a hash of the deleted installation identifier to reject old requests that could otherwise recreate these records. Summary counts without installation identifiers can remain.

Routine cleanup removes raw events after 90 days, installation records after 13 months without activity, and client-reported purchase records after 25 months. Your analytics choice leaves puzzle progress on your device. Apple and Google handle data they collect under their respective policies.

Google advertising and consent

The free game can display full-screen ads between puzzles and optional videos that earn hints. Google AdMob provides these ads. Google’s User Messaging Platform presents applicable privacy choices. Depending on your region, permissions, and choices, Google and its advertising partners may process the following data.

  • IP address and approximate location, including location inferred from IP address
  • Device or advertising identifiers, including Apple’s advertising identifier if you give tracking permission
  • App and device information, crash and other diagnostic data, and performance information
  • Ads shown, ad interactions such as video views, and other app interactions
  • Advertising privacy choices and information used to apply them

Google and its partners can use these data to deliver and measure ads, improve their services, and prevent fraud. With applicable permission, they can use these data to personalize ads and track activity across apps or websites. Eligible ads may still be served and measured if you decline personalization or tracking.

Choose Ad Privacy Choices in the game’s Settings when available. Manage Apple’s tracking permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Read Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Apple purchases

Apple processes payment for the one-time Remove Ads purchase and handles your payment credentials. Gilded Escape verifies the purchase with StoreKit so you can use and restore it. When you share usage analytics, purchase events can contain the information described above.

Remove Ads removes ads between puzzles and gives you hints without a video. Your usage analytics preference is managed in Settings. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you send to answer your request. You can contact us to ask about the data we hold, request correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. We may need more information to find the relevant installation or verify your request.

Changes to this policy

We’ll update this page and its effective date when our data practices change.
