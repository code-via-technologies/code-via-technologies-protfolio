Gilded Escape Support

How to play

  1. Slide a block along its length. Horizontal blocks move left and right, and vertical blocks move up and down.
  2. Move each block through open spaces. You can also tap a block and choose one of its available moves.
  3. Slide the gold block to the exit on the right.

Play at your own pace. A slide of any distance counts as one move. Undo puts the block back and lowers the move count by one. Restart returns the board and move count to the start.

Move targets and stars

Match the three-star target to earn three stars. If you go over, you earn two stars when you finish within the allowance of two moves or one third of the target rounded down, whichever is greater. Every other completion earns one star.

Daily Escape and streaks

A new daily puzzle becomes available at midnight in your device’s time zone. Players on the same calendar date get the same puzzle. Your streak counts one completion per date across consecutive dates. See your current and best streaks in Settings.

Hints

Tap Hint to find a move toward the solution. If the game offers a video, watch it through to unlock the hint. Hints are free with Remove Ads or when a video is unavailable.

Remove Ads and restore purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that removes ads between puzzles and gives you hints without watching a video. To restore it after reinstalling or changing devices, sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy it. Open the game’s Settings and choose Restore Purchases.

If a purchase is pending, wait for Apple’s confirmation. If the price fails to load, tap Load Price when your device is online. For billing questions or refunds, use Apple’s purchase support.

Saved progress

Level progress, stars, daily results, settings, and one unfinished puzzle are stored on your device. Removing the app can remove this progress.

Privacy choices

Turn off Share Usage Analytics in the game’s Settings to stop analytics collection and request deletion of prior usage data for this installation. The app retries a pending request when it can connect.

Use Ad Privacy Choices in the game’s Settings when available. Manage Apple’s tracking permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Gilded Escape privacy policy.
