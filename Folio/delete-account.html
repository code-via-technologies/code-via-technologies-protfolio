Delete your Folio account

Folio: Notes & Pins, by Code VIA Technologies · Last updated: October 30, 2026

The quickest way: delete your account inside the app. It takes effect immediately and works the same on Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Delete in the app

  • Android: open Folio, go to Settings and, in the account section, tap Delete account and confirm.
  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: open Folio, go to Settings > Your Account, tap Delete Account, type DELETE and confirm.

Can't use the app? Ask us by email

Email tom.weiss.t@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Folio account", from the email address you sign in to Folio with (or tell us which Google or Apple account you use). We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days, and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • The notes, folders and files you own, and their change history.
  • Your invitations, Folio Pro purchase records and device push tokens.
  • Your name and email. You are removed from notes other people shared with you.

What is kept

  • A "Deleted account" placeholder with no name or email, so the edit history of notes other people own stays consistent.
  • A one-way digest of your sign-in ID and the deletion time, so an old sign-in can't recreate the deleted account.
  • Your purchases themselves stay with Google Play or the App Store.
  • Notes kept only on your device or in your own iCloud are not on our servers. Delete them in the app or remove the app.

Details are in the Folio privacy policy.
