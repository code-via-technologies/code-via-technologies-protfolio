Privacy Policy for Folio

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Quick summary: Folio works without an account, and has no advertising. Without an account, your notes stay on your device and, if you enable iCloud, sync privately through your own Apple account. If you choose to sign in to a Folio account (to share notes or back them up), the notes in that account are stored on servers we operate so they can sync between your devices and the people you share with. Separately, the iPhone, iPad and Mac app sends anonymous usage analytics, on by default and off with one tap in Settings. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

What we collect

Without a Folio account: none of your notes. We cannot see your notes, photos, or attachments. That content never leaves your device except through your own iCloud sync.

With a Folio account: the account information and notes described in "Folio account" below. Signing in is optional, and only notes you keep in your Folio account library or share are stored with us.

Separately, Folio for iPhone, iPad and Mac sends anonymous usage analytics to a server we operate ourselves. This is a small, fixed set of events with no free-text fields. It never includes the content of your notes. Details are in the next section.

Usage analytics

Folio for iPhone, iPad and Mac sends anonymous, on-by-default usage analytics to a server we operate. It is a short list of events, and nothing else:

  • app_open: with a flag for whether it was a cold start
  • first_launch
  • purchase: product id, transaction id, and whether it was a purchase or a restore
  • paywall_view: where the paywall was shown from
  • feature_used: the name of a feature, from a fixed list we define
  • error: an error domain and a numeric code
  • opt_out: recorded when you turn analytics off

Alongside each event we record a random ID generated on your device when you first install the app (not an Apple device identifier, not shared with any other app, and not linked to your identity or to your Folio account), plus app version, iOS version, device model (e.g. "iPhone16,2"), language, your 2-letter country, and how many notes you currently have (a count only, never their titles or content). There are no free-text fields. Analytics never sees the text of your notes, your photos or attachments, or anything else you've typed.

We do not store IP addresses, even in logs; an IP is hashed with a key that rotates daily, used only to rate-limit abuse, and then discarded. Raw events are deleted after 90 days. Analytics never goes to an ad network, an analytics SDK, or any other third party. It goes to a server we operate ourselves and nowhere else. Nothing is sold or shared.

You can turn analytics off at any time in the app's Settings. Opting out stops new events from being sent and deletes the events already collected from your install. Folio for Android does not send usage analytics.

Folio account

A Folio account is optional. You need one to share notes, to back up your notes, and to use Folio Pro across devices. When you sign in, we store:

  • Your sign-in identity: an account identifier from the service you sign in with (Google or Apple), your name as that service provides it, and your verified email address. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only receive Apple's relay address.
  • Notes in your Folio account: the text, checklists, photos, files and folders of notes you keep in your Folio account library or share, plus who last edited each note and a recent history of changes so your devices can sync.
  • Invitations you send: the email address you invite, an optional message, and the invitation link. Invitations expire after 7 days.
  • Folio Pro purchases: which store you bought from (App Store or Google Play), the product, and the store's transaction reference, so Pro works on every device you sign in to. To confirm a purchase we check it with Apple or Google.
  • Your devices: a push notification token and the platform (iOS, macOS or Android) for each device you're signed in on.

Account data is encrypted in transit and note content is encrypted at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted: our server can read it in order to sync and share it. We use it only to run Folio. We don't look at your notes, sell them, use them for advertising, or use them to train AI models.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Notes and attached photos or files are stored locally on your device.
  • In your iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, Folio syncs your local library through Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud database: storage that belongs to your Apple account, protected by Apple, and inaccessible to us. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's own privacy policy.
  • On our servers, if you use a Folio account. Your account and the notes in it are stored in a database we operate, hosted by Railway in the European Union (the Netherlands).

Service providers

If you use a Folio account, these companies process data on our behalf, only to provide the service:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: signing you in with Google or Apple.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: delivering push notifications to your devices.
  • Railway: hosting our servers and database.
  • Apple and Google: confirming Folio Pro purchases made in their stores.
  • An email delivery provider: if we send invitation emails, only the recipient's address and the invitation go to it.

Sharing notes

You can share a note from your Folio account by inviting someone's email address or by creating a link. Only people who accept can see and edit the note. Everyone on a shared note can see the note, the names of the other people on it, and who last edited it. You can stop sharing at any time; the people you shared with then lose access.

Notifications and Live Activities

Note reminders and Lock Screen Live Activities are scheduled and rendered on your device. Their content never leaves your device except via your own iCloud sync.

If you use a Folio account, we send push notifications through Apple and Google. When someone shares a note with you, the notification shows their name and the note's title. Notifications that keep your devices in sync carry no note content.

AI features

"Enhance with AI" runs entirely on your device using Apple Intelligence. The text of your notes is never sent to us or to any third party by this feature.

Photos and files

When you attach a photo or file, Folio receives only the item you explicitly pick. It has no access to the rest of your photo library or file system.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Trash: notes you delete from your Folio account are permanently removed from our servers 30 days after they go to Trash, or right away if you choose "Delete permanently".
  • Deleting your account: you can delete your Folio account in the app's account settings. This permanently removes the notes, folders and files you own, your invitations, purchase records and device tokens, and removes you from notes others shared with you. Your name and email are erased; an anonymous "Deleted account" placeholder remains so the change history of notes shared with others stays consistent. Purchases themselves stay with Apple or Google.
  • Questions or requests: email us at the address below to access, correct or delete your data.

Children's Privacy

Folio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created a Folio account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If a future feature ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, with the effective date revised.
