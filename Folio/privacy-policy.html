Privacy Policy for Folio

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Quick summary: Folio works without an account, and has no advertising. Without an account, your notes stay on your device and, if you enable iCloud, sync privately through your own Apple account. If you choose to sign in to a Folio account (to share notes or back them up), the notes in that account are stored on servers we operate so they can sync between your devices and the people you share with. Separately, the iPhone, iPad and Mac app sends usage analytics, tied to a random install ID rather than your name, on by default and off with one tap in Settings. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

What we collect

Without a Folio account: none of your notes. We cannot see your notes, photos, or attachments. That content never leaves your device except through your own iCloud sync.

With a Folio account: the account information and notes described in "Folio account" below. Signing in is optional, and only notes you keep in your Folio account library or share are stored with us.

Separately, Folio for iPhone, iPad and Mac sends usage analytics to a server we operate ourselves. This is a small, fixed set of events with no free-text fields. It never includes the content of your notes. Details are in the next section.

Usage analytics

Folio for iPhone, iPad and Mac sends on-by-default usage analytics to a server we operate. It is a short list of events, and nothing else:

  • app_open: with a flag for whether it was a cold start
  • first_launch
  • purchase: product id, transaction id, and whether it was a purchase or a restore
  • paywall_view: where the paywall was shown from
  • feature_used: the name of a feature, from a fixed list we define
  • error: an error domain and a numeric code
  • opt_out: recorded when you turn analytics off

Alongside each event we record a random ID generated on your device when you first install the app (not an Apple device identifier, and not shared with any other app), plus app version, iOS version, device model (e.g. "iPhone16,2"), language, your 2-letter country, and how many notes you currently have (a count only, never their titles or content). There are no free-text fields. Analytics never sees the text of your notes, your photos or attachments, or anything else you've typed.

The install ID doesn't carry your name or email, and we don't join analytics to your Folio account. It is still personal data, though: a purchase event carries the store's transaction ID, which also appears in your Folio Pro purchase record if you have a Folio account, so the two could be matched. App Store privacy labels list analytics as linked to you for that reason.

We do not store IP addresses, even in logs; an IP is hashed with a key that rotates daily, used only to rate-limit abuse, and then discarded. Raw events are deleted after 90 days. A summary record per install (when it was first and last seen, app, iOS version and device model, language, country, how many times the app was opened, and whether it made a purchase) and the purchase records are kept until you ask us to delete them. Analytics never goes to an ad network, an analytics SDK, or any other third party. It goes to a server we operate ourselves and nowhere else. Nothing is sold or shared.

You can turn analytics off at any time in the app's Settings. Opting out stops new events from being sent. To delete what was already collected from your install, email us at the address below. Folio for Android does not send these usage analytics; the only diagnostics from it are Google's ML Kit reports when you use Enhance with AI (see "AI features").

Folio account

A Folio account is optional. You need one to share notes, to back up your notes, and to use Folio Pro across devices. When you sign in, we store:

  • Your sign-in identity: an account identifier from the service you sign in with (Google or Apple), your name as that service provides it, and your verified email address. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only receive Apple's relay address.
  • Notes in your Folio account: the text, checklists, photos, files and folders of notes you keep in your Folio account library or share, including any location you add to a note and any audio or video file you attach, plus who last edited each note and a recent history of changes so your devices can sync.
  • Invitations you send: the email address you invite, an optional message, and the invitation link. We don't email the invitation; it appears in the invited person's "Shared with me" when they sign in with that address. Invitations expire after 7 days.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report a shared note or a person, we store who reported it, which note and person, the reason you picked, the details you wrote (encrypted at rest) and the note's revision, so we can review it. If you block someone, we store that you blocked them. We also record which version of the sharing terms you accepted and when.
  • Folio Pro purchases: which store you bought from (App Store or Google Play), the product, and the store's transaction reference, so Pro works on every device you sign in to. To confirm a purchase we check it with Apple or Google.
  • Your devices: a push notification token and the platform (iOS, macOS or Android) for each device you're signed in on.

Account data is encrypted in transit and note content is encrypted at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted: our server can read it in order to sync and share it. We use it only to run Folio. We don't look at your notes, sell them, use them for advertising, or use them to train AI models.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Notes and attached photos or files are stored locally on your device.
  • In your iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, Folio syncs your local library through Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud database: storage that belongs to your Apple account, protected by Apple, and inaccessible to us. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's own privacy policy.
  • On our servers, if you use a Folio account. Your account and the notes in it are stored in a database we operate, hosted by Railway in the European Union (the Netherlands).

Service providers

If you use a Folio account, these companies process data on our behalf, only to provide the service:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: signing you in with Google or Apple.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: delivering push notifications to your devices.
  • Railway: hosting our servers and database.
  • Apple and Google: confirming Folio Pro purchases made in their stores.
  • Google ML Kit (Android): running Enhance with AI on your phone. It sends Google diagnostics when you use the feature, as described under "AI features".

Sharing notes

You can share a note from your Folio account by inviting someone's email address or by creating a link. Only people who accept can see and edit the note. Everyone on a shared note can see the note, the names of the other people on it, and who last edited it. You can stop sharing at any time; the people you shared with then lose access.

Notifications and Live Activities

Note reminders and Lock Screen Live Activities are scheduled and rendered on your device. Their content never leaves your device except via your own iCloud sync.

If you use a Folio account, we send push notifications through Apple and Google. When someone shares a note with you, the notification shows their name and the note's title. Notifications that keep your devices in sync carry no note content.

AI features

"Enhance with AI" runs entirely on your device: on iPhone, iPad and Mac it uses Apple Intelligence, and on Android it uses Google's on-device Gemini Nano model, which runs through Android's AICore system service. The text of your notes is never sent to us or to any third party by this feature. It only works on devices that support these on-device models; elsewhere the app explains why it isn't available.

On Android, Folio Pro users can tap Enhance with AI. When they do, Google's ML Kit library, which connects Folio to the on-device model, sends Google diagnostic information: device and app information, performance metrics, feature usage and error codes, configured languages, and identifiers. Google uses it for diagnostics and usage analytics. It never includes the text of your notes, and nothing is sent unless you use the feature.

Photos, files and locations

When you attach a photo or file, Folio receives only the item you explicitly pick. It has no access to the rest of your photo library or file system.

When you add your current location to a note, Folio asks for location access while you use the app and reads your position once, at that moment. The location is saved in the note like any other content: on your device, in your iCloud, and on our servers only if the note is in your Folio account. Folio never tracks your location in the background.

Feedback

If you use Send Feedback in the iPhone, iPad or Mac app, we receive your message with the app and system versions. It is stored on the same server as analytics and forwarded to our private Slack workspace so we can read it. Slack stores it on our behalf. Feedback is kept until you ask us to delete it; it isn't tied to your account, so tell us roughly what you wrote and when.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Trash: notes you delete from your Folio account are permanently removed from our servers 30 days after they go to Trash, or right away if you choose "Delete permanently".
  • Deleting your account: you can delete your Folio account in the app's account settings. This permanently removes the notes, folders and files you own, your invitations, purchase records and device tokens, and removes you from notes others shared with you. Your name and email are erased; a "Deleted account" placeholder remains so the change history of notes shared with others stays consistent. Purchases themselves stay with Apple or Google.
  • Questions or requests: email us at the address below to access, correct or delete your data.

Children's Privacy

Folio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created a Folio account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If a future feature ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, with the effective date revised.
