Privacy Policy for Folio

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Quick summary: Folio works without an account, and has no advertising. Without an account, your notes stay on your device and, if you enable iCloud, sync privately through your own Apple account. If you choose to sign in to a Folio account (to share notes or back them up), the notes in that account are stored on servers we operate so they can sync between your devices and the people you share with. Separately, the iPhone, iPad and Mac app sends usage analytics, tied to a random install ID rather than your name: on by default, except in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland where it asks first, and off with one tap in Settings. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Who we are

Folio is published by Code VIA Technologies ("we", "us"), which is responsible for the personal data described here (the "controller" under European data protection law). You can reach us at the email address at the end of this policy. Using Folio is also subject to our Terms of Service.

What we collect

Without a Folio account: none of your notes. We cannot see your notes, photos, or attachments. That content never leaves your device except through your own iCloud sync.

With a Folio account: the account information and notes described in "Folio account" below. Signing in is optional, and only notes you keep in your Folio account library or share are stored with us.

Separately, Folio for iPhone, iPad and Mac sends usage analytics to a server we operate ourselves. This is a small, fixed set of events with no free-text fields. It never includes the content of your notes. Details are in the next section.

Usage analytics

Folio for iPhone, iPad and Mac sends on-by-default usage analytics to a server we operate. It is a short list of events, and nothing else:

  • app_open: with a flag for whether it was a cold start
  • first_launch
  • purchase: product id, transaction id, and whether it was a purchase or a restore
  • paywall_view: where the paywall was shown from
  • feature_used: the name of a feature, from a fixed list we define
  • error: an error domain and a numeric code
  • opt_out: recorded when you turn analytics off

Alongside each event we record a random ID generated on your device when you first install the app (not an Apple device identifier, and not shared with any other app), plus app version, iOS version, device model (e.g. "iPhone16,2"), language, your 2-letter country, and how many notes you currently have (a count only, never their titles or content). There are no free-text fields. Analytics never sees the text of your notes, your photos or attachments, or anything else you've typed.

The install ID doesn't carry your name or email, and we don't join analytics to your Folio account. It is still personal data, though: a purchase event carries the store's transaction ID, which also appears in your Folio Pro purchase record if you have a Folio account, so the two could be matched. App Store privacy labels list analytics as linked to you for that reason.

We do not store IP addresses, even in logs; an IP is hashed with a key that rotates daily, used only to rate-limit abuse, and then discarded. Raw events are deleted after 90 days. A summary record per install (when it was first and last seen, app, iOS version and device model, language, country, how many times the app was opened, and whether it made a purchase) and the purchase records are kept until you ask us to delete them. Analytics never goes to an ad network or an analytics SDK. It goes to a server we operate ourselves. To keep an eye on the service, that server also posts a short alert to our private Slack workspace when a new install or a purchase is first seen, with the device model, iOS version, app version, country, and the product and whether it was a purchase or a restore. Slack stores these alerts on our behalf. Nothing is sold or shared.

In the EU, the rest of the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland (based on your device's Region setting), from Folio 1.7.0 build 212 analytics are off until you choose "Allow" when the app asks; nothing is sent and no install ID is created before that. You can turn analytics off at any time in the app's Settings. Opting out stops new events from being sent. To delete what was already collected, tap "Delete analytics data" in Settings: our server deletes the events, purchase records and install record linked to your install ID. If the server can't be reached, the app keeps retrying while it's open, and Settings shows "Retry analytics deletion" until the server confirms. You can also email us at the address below. Aggregate counts that no longer identify an install are kept. Folio for Android does not send these usage analytics; the only diagnostics from it are Google's ML Kit reports when you use Enhance with AI (see "AI features").

Folio account

A Folio account is optional. You need one to share notes, to back up your notes, and to use Folio Pro across devices. When you sign in, we store:

  • Your sign-in identity: an account identifier from the service you sign in with (Google or Apple), your name as that service provides it, and your verified email address. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only receive Apple's relay address.
  • Notes in your Folio account: the text, checklists, photos, files and folders of notes you keep in your Folio account library or share, including any location you add to a note and any audio or video file you attach, plus who last edited each note and a recent history of changes so your devices can sync.
  • Invitations you send: the email address you invite, an optional message, and the invitation link. We don't email the invitation; it appears in the invited person's "Shared with me" when they sign in with that address. Invitations expire after 7 days.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report a shared note or a person, we store who reported it, which note and person, the reason you picked, the details you wrote (encrypted at rest) and the note's revision, so we can review it. A report about an account is kept for up to one year after we resolve it, even if that account is deleted, so we can handle repeated abuse. If you block someone, we store that you blocked them. We also record which version of the sharing terms you accepted and when.
  • Folio Pro purchases: which store you bought from (App Store or Google Play), the product, and the store's transaction reference, so Pro works on every device you sign in to. To confirm a purchase we check it with Apple or Google.
  • Your devices: a push notification token and the platform (iOS, macOS or Android) for each device you're signed in on.

Account data is encrypted in transit and note content is encrypted at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted: our server can read it in order to sync and share it. We use it only to run Folio. We don't look at your notes, sell them, use them for advertising, or use them to train AI models.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Notes and attached photos or files are stored locally on your device.
  • In your iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, Folio syncs your local library through Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud database: storage that belongs to your Apple account, protected by Apple, and inaccessible to us. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's own privacy policy.
  • On our servers, if you use a Folio account. Your account and the notes in it are stored in a database we operate, hosted by Railway in the European Union (the Netherlands).
  • In your Google backup, on Android. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android includes Folio's notes and attachments in your Google device backup and device-to-device transfers. Google handles that backup under your Google account; we can't see it. Your Folio sign-in is never included.

Service providers

If you use a Folio account, these companies process data on our behalf, only to provide the service:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: signing you in with Google or Apple.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: delivering push notifications to your devices.
  • Railway: hosting our servers and database.
  • Apple and Google: confirming Folio Pro purchases made in their stores.
  • Google ML Kit (Android): running Enhance with AI on your phone. It sends Google diagnostics when you use the feature, as described under "AI features".
  • Slack: receiving feedback you send and the analytics alerts described under "Usage analytics", in our private workspace.

International transfers

Our database is hosted in the European Union. Some of the providers above (Google, Apple, Slack and Railway) are based in the United States and may process data there. Where data protection law requires it, these transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as provided in each provider's data processing terms. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

Sharing notes

You can share a note from your Folio account by inviting someone's email address or by creating a link. Only people who accept can see and edit the note. Everyone on a shared note can see the note, the names of the other people on it, and who last edited it. You can stop sharing at any time; the people you shared with then lose access.

Notifications and Live Activities

Note reminders and Lock Screen Live Activities are scheduled and rendered on your device. Their content never leaves your device except via your own iCloud sync.

If you use a Folio account, we send push notifications through Apple and Google. When someone shares a note with you, the notification shows their name, not the note's title. Notifications that keep your devices in sync carry no note content.

AI features

"Enhance with AI" runs entirely on your device: on iPhone, iPad and Mac it uses Apple Intelligence, and on Android it uses Google's on-device Gemini Nano model, which runs through Android's AICore system service. The text of your notes is never sent to us or to any third party by this feature. It only works on devices that support these on-device models; elsewhere the app explains why it isn't available.

On Android, Folio Pro users can tap Enhance with AI. When they do, Google's ML Kit library, which connects Folio to the on-device model, sends Google diagnostic information: device and app information, performance metrics, feature usage and error codes, configured languages, and identifiers. Google uses it for diagnostics and usage analytics. It never includes the text of your notes, and nothing is sent unless you use the feature.

Photos, files and locations

When you attach a photo or file, Folio receives only the item you explicitly pick. It has no access to the rest of your photo library or file system.

When you add your current location to a note, Folio asks for location access while you use the app and reads your position once, at that moment. The location is saved in the note like any other content: on your device, in your iCloud, and on our servers only if the note is in your Folio account. Folio never tracks your location in the background.

Feedback

If you use Send Feedback in the iPhone, iPad or Mac app, we receive your message with the app and system versions. It is stored on the same server as analytics and forwarded to our private Slack workspace so we can read it. Slack stores it on our behalf. Feedback is kept until you ask us to delete it; it isn't tied to your account, so tell us roughly what you wrote and when.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Trash: notes you delete from your Folio account are permanently removed from our servers 30 days after they go to Trash, or right away if you choose "Delete permanently".
  • Deleting your account: you can delete your Folio account in the app's account settings. This permanently removes the notes, folders and files you own, your invitations, purchase records and device tokens, and removes you from notes others shared with you. Your name and email are erased; a "Deleted account" placeholder remains so the change history of notes shared with others stays consistent. We also keep a one-way digest of your sign-in ID and the deletion time, so an old sign-in can't recreate the deleted account, and reports about your account as described under "Reports and blocks". Purchases themselves stay with Apple or Google. Deleting your account doesn't delete usage analytics, which aren't tied to your account; you can ask us to delete those separately.
  • How long we keep data: account data and the notes in your account are kept until you delete them or your account. Invitations are removed 90 days after they expire or are answered. Device push tokens are removed when you sign out or delete your account. Resolved reports are kept for up to one year. Raw analytics events are kept for 90 days; analytics summaries, purchase records and feedback are kept until you ask us to delete them. Copies in backups are removed as those backups expire.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide Folio to you (performance of our contract with you): your account, syncing and backing up the notes in your account, sharing, notifications, and confirming Folio Pro purchases.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping Folio secure, preventing abuse and handling reports and blocks, understanding how the app is used through usage analytics (in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, only with your consent), and reading feedback you send. You can object at any time; for analytics, turning them off in Settings stops collection.
  • Legal obligations: keeping records we are required to keep and responding to lawful requests.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, give you a copy in a portable format, restrict how we use it, or stop using it (object). Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. You can export your notes from the app yourself, and delete your account in the app. For anything else, email us at the address below; we reply within one month. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, such as the supervisory authority in your EU country, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or Israel's Privacy Protection Authority.

California residents

We don't sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. You have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and we won't discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Email us to make a request; an authorized agent can make one on your behalf.

Children's Privacy

Folio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created a Folio account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
