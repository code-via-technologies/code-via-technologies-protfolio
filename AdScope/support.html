AdScope support

Connect your account

Choose Connect Google account and use the Google account associated with your AdMob publisher account. AdScope requests read-only reporting access. You can explore sample data without connecting.

Understanding the numbers

Earnings are estimates and may be delayed or adjusted by Google. AdScope uses the publisher account’s currency and reporting time zone. It currently includes AdMob Network reports, not third-party mediation revenue or finalized payments. Status describes the connection and report freshness, not Google policy or app-readiness decisions.

Remove Ads subscription

Remove Ads is an optional monthly auto-renewable subscription, priced at $0.99/month in the US. Your local price is shown before purchase. It removes all AdScope banner ads while active. All dashboard features are also available free.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Access continues until the end of the paid period. Open Remove Ads in Settings to restore purchases or manage your subscription, or use Apple subscription settings.

Disconnect or change privacy choices

Open Settings → Disconnect & erase local reports to remove the cached report and revoke the connection. Ad privacy choices are available in Settings when required. Disconnecting or deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.
