AdScope · Support · Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026. AdScope is provided by Code VIA Technologies. Contact: tom.weiss.t@gmail.com.

Google sign-in and AdMob reports

When you connect Google, AdScope requests read-only AdMob reporting and app-details access (admob.report and admob.readonly). It uses your publisher account, currency, reporting time zone, app identifiers, app names, and report metrics to show earnings and performance. Google Sign-In handles basic account identity and credentials to authenticate your connection.

Reports travel directly from Google to your iPhone over HTTPS. We do not operate a server that receives your reports or Google access tokens. We do not sell, transfer to advertisers, or use Google account or report data for advertising, credit decisions, or training AI models. AdScope’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

App icons

AdScope uses public App Store identifiers linked to your iOS apps in AdMob to request app icons from Apple. Icon requests do not include Google access tokens or earnings. Apple and its image-delivery providers receive normal network information, such as your IP address. Icon links are cached locally with iOS file protection and excluded from backups; disconnecting Google removes them. Apps without an available linked App Store listing show a placeholder.

Storage and deletion

The most recent report is cached on your device using iOS file protection and excluded from backups. Google Sign-In manages credentials in the device keychain. In Settings, “Disconnect & erase local reports” revokes the connection and deletes the cached report. You can also revoke access through Google account connections. Deleting AdScope removes its local app data; revoke Google access separately if needed.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free version displays Google AdMob banner ads. Google and its advertising partners may process IP address (including approximate location derived from it), device and app identifiers, diagnostics, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. AdScope requests non-personalized ads; those ads still involve data processing. Your AdMob reporting data and Google access tokens are never attached to ad requests.

Where required, Google’s consent interface asks for privacy choices before ads are requested. Available choices can be revisited from Settings. An active Remove Ads subscription removes banners and stops ad requests. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Subscriptions

Apple processes payments. AdScope reads verified App Store transaction and subscription status to determine ad-free access. We do not receive your payment-card details. Deleting the app or disconnecting Google does not cancel a subscription; manage it in your Apple Account.

Support and children

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond. Please do not send Google credentials or full financial reports. AdScope is a publisher utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and requests

We update this page when practices change and will request new consent where required. Contact the email above for privacy questions or deletion requests concerning support correspondence.
