AdScope · Support · Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026. AdScope is provided by Code VIA Technologies. Contact: tom.weiss.t@gmail.com.

Google sign-in and AdMob reports

When you connect Google, AdScope requests read-only AdMob reporting and app-details access (admob.report and admob.readonly). It uses your publisher account, currency, reporting time zone, app identifiers, app names, and report metrics to show earnings and performance. Google Sign-In handles basic account identity and credentials to authenticate your connection.

Reports travel directly from Google to your iPhone over HTTPS. We do not operate a server that receives your reports or Google access tokens. We do not sell, transfer to advertisers, or use Google account or report data for advertising, credit decisions, or training AI models. AdScope’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

App icons

AdScope uses public App Store identifiers linked to your iOS apps in AdMob to request app icons from Apple. Icon requests do not include Google access tokens or earnings. Apple and its image-delivery providers receive normal network information, such as your IP address. Icon links are cached locally with iOS file protection and excluded from backups; disconnecting Google removes them. Apps without an available linked App Store listing show a placeholder.

Storage and deletion

The most recent report is cached on your device using iOS file protection and excluded from backups. Google Sign-In manages credentials in the device keychain. In Settings, “Disconnect & erase local reports” revokes the connection and deletes the cached report. You can also revoke access through Google account connections. Deleting AdScope removes its local app data; revoke Google access separately if needed.

Optional usage analytics

Versions with the “Share usage analytics” setting send basic usage events to our developer-operated analytics service hosted on Railway. Sharing is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings. Events cover app opens, screen visits, demo use, reporting-period choices, and the outcomes of Google connection, report refresh, and subscription flows. Subscription-flow events are usage signals, not sales or revenue records.

Events contain fixed labels and a random identifier for this installation, along with app version, operating-system version, device model, language, and the country from your device region setting. This identifier is not an advertising identifier, is not shared across apps, and is not linked to your Google account. We do not include earnings, report contents, app or publisher identifiers, Google identity or credentials, transaction identifiers, search text, or error messages. We do not use these events for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Raw usage events are retained for up to 90 days. Installation metadata and aggregate usage statistics may remain longer. Turn off “Share usage analytics” or choose “Delete usage data” in Settings to stop collection and request deletion of this installation’s usage records. If you are offline, deletion is retained on your device and retried when a connection is available; collection stays paused while deletion is pending. Deleting the app alone does not send a server deletion request, so use the in-app control first if you want to erase usage records. Analytics does not require Google sign-in or access to your reports.

No advertising or cross-app tracking

AdScope contains no advertising SDK, does not request the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. There are no banner ads or advertising consent forms.

Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In remains necessary to connect your publisher account. Google’s SDK privacy disclosure covers account identity and contact information, approximate location, user and device identifiers, usage data, and other data for sign-in functionality and SDK analytics. These are not used by AdScope for advertising or tracking. See Google’s privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Apple processes payments. AdScope reads verified App Store transaction and subscription status to unlock your real dashboard while your subscription is active. We do not receive your payment-card details. Deleting the app or disconnecting Google does not cancel a subscription; manage it in your Apple Account.

Support and children

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond. Please do not send Google credentials or full financial reports. AdScope is a publisher utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and requests

We update this page when practices change and will request new consent where required. Contact the email above for privacy questions or deletion requests concerning support correspondence.
