AdScope is a native iPhone dashboard for publishers who use Google AdMob. Connect your Google account to view AdMob Network earnings, impressions, match rate, and eCPM across your apps.

Made for a quick check-in

  • Explore earnings trends and compare completed reporting periods.
  • See app-level performance and report freshness.
  • Keep your latest report available offline, with an optional Face ID or passcode lock.
  • Explore clearly labeled sample data before connecting.

AdScope is preparing for its first App Store release. AdScope Monthly is $0.99/month in the US; local prices vary. An active subscription is required to connect your Google account and view your reports. There are no ads or free plan. Explore the sample-data demo before subscribing.

Reports cover AdMob Network estimates. Third-party mediation revenue, finalized payments, and Google policy or app-readiness status are not included.

Your Google data stays yours

Read-only AdMob reports are fetched directly from Google to your device. They are not sent to a developer server or used to target advertising. Read our privacy policy.
