Privacy Policy for Gilded Peaks Solitaire

Effective November 16, 2026

No game account is required. Your saved records stay on your device. We collect limited usage data to improve the app, and Google may process advertising data when ads are available. You can turn off developer usage collection and request deletion in Settings.

Local game data

Round summaries, wins, personal bests, daily completions, streaks and settings are stored on your device. These saved records are not uploaded as a database or synced through a game account. Deleting the app removes its local data under normal iOS behavior; Apple backup settings may also apply.

Developer usage data

We receive limited event data through our telemetry service hosted by Railway. Each installation uses a randomly generated identifier, separate from the advertising identifier and from identifiers used by other apps. Events also have an identifier and timestamp.

  • App opens and first launch; starting and finishing rounds; daily starts and completions; hint offers, use and earned rewards; actual full-screen ad impressions; and visits to the remove-ads option.
  • Verified purchase or restore events, including the product identifier, Apple transaction identifier and transaction type. We do not receive your card details or billing address.
  • App version, iOS version, device model, language and country or region code.

We use this information to understand usage, reliability and which features need improvement. We do not send card layouts, deal seeds, move histories, round duration, names, email addresses or precise location through this telemetry. The installation identifier makes these records pseudonymous; it does not make every event unidentifiable to that installation. Network services necessarily process IP addresses to deliver requests.

Usage controls, retention and deletion

In Settings, turn off Share usage analytics to stop developer usage collection and request deletion of the installation’s server records. You can also select Delete usage data. The app retains a local deletion request until the server confirms it. If you are offline or the service is unavailable, it retries when connectivity returns or the app is reopened. Collection remains blocked while deletion is pending. Keep the app installed and reopen it online so a pending request can finish.

Raw usage events are retained for up to 90 days unless deleted sooner. Deleting developer usage data does not delete your local game records, cancel your Apple purchase or delete information held independently by Google or Apple. After confirmed deletion, usage collection can resume if you choose to enable it.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for optional rewarded hints and occasional full-screen ads after completed rounds. Google’s User Messaging Platform manages applicable privacy messages. The app requests ads only when Google’s consent status permits it. Where required, you can reopen Ad privacy choices in Settings.

Google and its advertising partners may process IP-derived general location, app and device identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash logs and performance information to deliver and measure advertising and prevent fraud. The app does not request access to precise device location and uses Google’s privacy messaging to request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission from eligible users before advertising where applicable. You can decline tracking; gameplay remains available and eligible ads may still be shown. You can change an existing tracking permission in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking. Advertising data practices and consent choices are separate from the developer usage controls above.

See Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps using its services, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Purchases

Apple handles the optional one-time remove-ads purchase. The app verifies the entitlement to remove all ads and make hints free. Restore purchases is available in Settings. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Removing ads does not automatically turn off developer usage collection; that has its own control.

Support and your rights

If you contact us, we receive the information you include in your email and use it to answer your request. Do not include payment details or other sensitive information. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information. Use the in-app deletion control for installation-linked usage records, or contact us for help.
