Gilded Peaks Solitaire Support

How to play

  • Clear all 28 cards from the three peaks to win.
  • Tap an exposed card one rank above or below the waste card. Suits do not matter. Ace connects to both King and 2.
  • A covered card becomes available when both cards below it have been removed.
  • Tap the stock to draw a new waste card. The stock does not recycle, and drawing ends your current run.
  • Undo is available during play and after a win or loss, until you choose View result.

The daily climb

A solvable daily deal follows your device’s local Gregorian date. Complete it to build a daily streak. Replaying a completed day does not add another streak day. Some daily deals may repeat.

Hints and card display

A hint highlights a legal card or the stock. When offered, completing a short rewarded ad unlocks the hint. If no ad is available, the hint is free. Open Card display options during a round for Larger cards or Exposed cards as a list. In larger-card mode, swipe horizontally to see the whole board.

Remove ads and restore purchases

The optional one-time purchase removes all ads and makes hints free. Open Settings to check the current price or choose Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple handles payments and refund requests.

Saved records and unfinished rounds

Saved wins, streaks and bests stay on this device. An unfinished round survives backgrounding but is discarded when the app process closes. Time begins with your first move and pauses while the app is in the background or an ad is presented.

Usage data and ad privacy

Settings contains Usage analytics and Delete usage data controls for developer telemetry. If deletion is pending, reopen the app online to let it finish. Ad privacy choices, when required by Google, are separate. Read the Privacy Policy for the data collected and your options.
