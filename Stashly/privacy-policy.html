Privacy Policy for Stashly

Effective August 2026

Quick summary: Stashly contains no networking code. It has no account system, no server, and no analytics. Nothing you save ever leaves your device, because there is nothing in the app capable of sending it anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Stashly does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app makes no network calls of any kind — no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, and no advertising SDK.

Your clips and snippets

Text, images, and files you save are stored in Stashly's own private container on your device, shared only between the Stashly app and its own keyboard and Share extensions. They are not synced to iCloud or any other service, and they are not readable by other apps.

The keyboard and "Allow Full Access"

Stashly includes a keyboard extension. iOS requires that you grant it Allow Full Access before it can read the clips you saved, because those clips live in a shared container and iOS gates shared-container access behind that permission.

iOS warns that a keyboard with Full Access "can access all the data you type." That warning is shown for every third-party keyboard. For Stashly specifically:

  • Stashly does not record, log, or store what you type.
  • Stashly stores only the items you explicitly choose to save.
  • Stashly has no network capability, so it cannot transmit anything anywhere.

Your clipboard

Stashly can only read your clipboard when you deliberately tap the system Paste button inside the keyboard, or send content to Stashly through the iOS Share Sheet. It cannot and does not monitor your clipboard in the background — iOS does not permit any app to do that.

Purchases

Stashly Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit. Stashly never sees or stores your payment information.

Children's Privacy

Stashly does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If a future feature ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, with the effective date revised.
