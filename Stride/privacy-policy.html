Privacy Policy for Stride

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Quick summary: Stride has no accounts. Your step data stays on your iPhone, and Health access is read-only — Stride never writes to your Apple Health data. Separately, the app sends anonymous usage analytics — on by default, off with one tap in Settings — to a server we operate. See "Usage analytics" below for exactly what that is.

What we collect

Your step and health data: nothing. Stride does not have accounts or advertising. We cannot see your step counts, walks, or goals — that data never leaves your device.

Separately, Stride sends anonymous usage analytics to a server we operate ourselves. This is a small, fixed set of events with no free-text fields — it never includes any of the health or location data described below. Details are in the next section.

Usage analytics

Stride sends anonymous, on-by-default usage analytics to a server we operate. It is a short list of events, and nothing else:

  • app_open — with a flag for whether it was a cold start
  • first_launch
  • feature_used — the name of a feature, from a fixed list we define (e.g. starting a walk or workout)
  • error — an error domain and a numeric code
  • opt_out — recorded when you turn analytics off

Alongside each event we record a random ID generated on your device when you first install the app — not an Apple device identifier, not shared with any other app, and not linked to your identity — plus app version, iOS version, device model (e.g. "iPhone16,2"), language, and your 2-letter country. There are no free-text fields. Analytics never sees your step counts, walks, goals, or location.

We do not store IP addresses, even in logs; an IP is hashed with a key that rotates daily, used only to rate-limit abuse, and then discarded. Raw events are deleted after 90 days. Analytics never goes to an ad network, an analytics SDK, or any other third party — it goes to a server we operate ourselves and nowhere else. Nothing is sold or shared.

You can turn analytics off at any time in the app's Settings. Opting out stops new events from being sent and deletes the events already collected from your install.

Apple Health

Stride reads your daily step total from Apple Health so it can show your progress on the Lock Screen tile and in the Dynamic Island. This access is read-only — Stride never writes, modifies, or deletes any data in Apple Health. You can revoke Health access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Location

When you start a walk, Stride uses your location only to keep step and distance tracking active while the app is in the background or the screen is locked. Location data is processed entirely on your device to power the live Dynamic Island and Lock Screen updates during a walk — it is never transmitted anywhere, stored after the walk ends, or used for any other purpose.

Where your data lives

Step counts, walk history, and your daily goal are stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is synced to iCloud or any other service, and nothing leaves your device.

Live Activities and Lock Screen

The Dynamic Island and Lock Screen tile are rendered on your device by iOS using data Stride already has locally. This content never leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

Stride does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If a future feature ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, with the effective date revised.
