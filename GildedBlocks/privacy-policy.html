Privacy Policy for Gilded Blocks

Effective November 17, 2026

Gilded Blocks is published by Code VIA Technologies. Your game progress is stored on your device. We collect basic usage analytics to improve the game, with an in-app opt-out and deletion control. Google provides eligible ads and consent messages, and Apple handles purchases. These services have separate data practices and choices.

What stays on your device

Your scores, daily history, unfinished round and settings are stored locally. The app has no game account or cloud progress sync. We do not send your board layout or saved game to our telemetry service. Deleting the app may remove local progress, but does not itself request deletion of previously collected server data.

First-party usage analytics

Usage analytics are on by default. The app sends a fixed set of events to a portfolio telemetry service we operate, hosted on Railway: first launch, app opens, gameplay and daily-play feature use, game outcomes, ad impressions and earned continues, paywall views, verified purchases or restores, and operational error codes. This helps us understand reliability, feature use and whether players return.

Events include a random installation identifier generated by this app, event identifiers and timestamps, app version, iOS version, device model, language and two-letter country from device settings. Purchase events can include the product identifier, Apple transaction identifier and purchase or restore type. The installation identifier is not IDFA or IDFV and is not shared across our apps. We do not include names, email addresses, free-text content, precise location or payment details in these events.

The telemetry endpoint receives an IP address as part of connecting to the service. Our application uses a daily rotating keyed hash for abuse prevention rather than storing the raw address in analytics records. Hosting providers may process connection and security logs to operate their services. Debug builds and automated tests do not send production usage analytics.

Analytics choices and deletion

In Settings, turn off Share usage analytics to stop collection and request deletion of the first-party usage data associated with this installation. Delete usage data requests deletion without permanently turning off future collection. To keep analytics off, also turn off the sharing toggle.

Deletion removes installation-linked events, purchase telemetry and the installation record from the active telemetry database after server confirmation. If the device is offline or the service is unavailable, the app keeps the request pending, retries it and blocks new collection while erasure is pending. A pending message does not mean deletion is complete. Use the app’s deletion control before uninstalling so the app can identify the installation’s data. You can also contact us about a privacy request.

Raw analytics events are retained for 90 days. Installation records are retained for up to 13 months after last activity, and client-reported purchase telemetry for up to 25 months, unless deleted earlier. Aggregate statistics that no longer identify an installation may remain. Restricted backups may retain deleted data until their normal expiry. These controls do not delete Apple purchase records or data held by Google.

Google advertising and consent

The free game can display Google AdMob banner ads, full-screen ads between rounds and optional rewarded video continues. Google’s User Messaging Platform manages applicable privacy messages and consent choices. Depending on your region, permissions and consent, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device or advertising identifiers, including IDFA only with Apple tracking permission;
  • device and app information, including operating system and app version;
  • ads shown, ad interactions and other product interactions;
  • crash, diagnostic and performance information; and
  • consent choices and information needed to display and manage privacy messages.

This information can be used for delivering and measuring advertising, personalization where permitted, analytics, fraud prevention and service functionality. Advertising data may be linked to device identifiers and used for tracking across apps or websites when permitted. Declining personalization or Apple tracking permission does not necessarily stop all advertising data processing or all eligible ads.

Use Advertising privacy choices in the app’s Settings when available to revisit applicable consent choices. Manage Apple tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. These are separate from Share usage analytics: changing one does not automatically change the other. You can keep playing if ads or consent services are unavailable.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps using its services. Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure describes its SDK data practices.

Purchases

Apple processes the one-time Remove Ads purchase through StoreKit. We do not receive your card number or payment credentials. The app verifies transactions and purchase entitlements to provide and restore the purchased features. Our usage analytics may record the product, transaction identifier and purchase or restore type as described above.

Remove Ads removes banner and between-round ads and makes optional continues free. It does not automatically turn off first-party usage analytics, delete prior data or erase Apple’s purchase records. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for Apple’s practices.

Service providers, security and your rights

We use hosting and infrastructure providers to operate the telemetry service. Google and Apple process data as described above. Providers may process data in countries other than your own. We use encrypted network connections and restrict access to operational data, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy regulator. Contact us to make a request. We may need information to locate the relevant installation and verify a request; please do not send passwords or payment details.

Children

Gilded Blocks is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information so we can investigate and address it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when the app’s data practices change.
