Gilded Blocks

DAILY PUZZLES. GOLDEN MOMENTS.

A quieter kind of puzzle.

Arrange warm gold pieces on a midnight board. Fill rows and columns, make space, and see how far a thoughtful placement can take you.

Find your flow

Tap or drag pieces onto an 8 × 8 board. Clear full rows and columns together. No timer, rotation or undo.

A daily arrangement

Try the same 30-piece sequence as everyone else, refreshed at midnight UTC. A round ends when no piece fits.

Keep playing

Play an endless round at your own pace. Your scores, daily history and unfinished round stay on your device.

Made for thoughtful play

VoiceOver labels, scalable text and a large-button placement list give you more ways to play.

Free to play, with a one-time ad-free option

The free game can show banner and between-round ads. Endless rounds offer one optional video continue that clears the board and keeps your score. If an ad cannot load, the continue is free. Daily arrangements have no continue.

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that removes banner and between-round ads and makes optional continues free. Purchases and restores are handled by Apple.
