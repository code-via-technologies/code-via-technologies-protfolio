Gilded Blocks Support

How to play

  • Tap or drag a piece from the tray onto the 8 × 8 board. A piece must fit completely in empty squares.
  • Fill a row or column to clear it. Rows and columns completed by the same placement clear together.
  • Pieces keep their orientation. There is no rotation, undo or timer.
  • The round ends when none of the remaining tray pieces fits.

Daily arrangements

Everyone gets the same 30-piece sequence for the UTC date. It refreshes at midnight UTC, which may differ from your local midnight. A round can end before all 30 pieces if none fits. Daily streaks count dates played, once per UTC date. Daily rounds have no continue.

Continuing an endless round

An endless round offers one optional video continue. It clears the board and keeps your score. If an ad cannot load, the continue is free. Players with Remove Ads also get this continue free.

Remove Ads and restore purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that removes banner and between-round ads and makes optional continues free. To restore it after reinstalling or changing phones, sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase, then open the game’s Settings and choose Restore purchases. An internet connection may be needed.

If a purchase is pending approval, wait for Apple’s confirmation. For billing or refund help, use Apple’s purchase support. Contact us if the game still does not recognize a completed purchase.

Saved games and scores

Scores, daily history, settings and your unfinished round are stored on this device. There is no game account or cloud game sync. Removing the app can remove this local progress; restoring a purchase does not restore scores.

Usage analytics and advertising choices

In Settings, turn off Share usage analytics to stop first-party usage collection and request deletion of prior usage data for this installation. Delete usage data requests deletion separately. If offline, deletion stays pending and retries when a connection is available; the app confirms when it is complete.

Advertising consent is separate. Use Advertising privacy choices in Settings when available, and manage Apple tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. These choices do not prevent you from playing. Read our privacy policy for details.
