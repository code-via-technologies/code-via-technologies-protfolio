What Napling Does

Napling lets you log feedings, sleep, diaper changes, growth measurements, and milestones in seconds, and see them as a clear timeline and trend charts. Everything lives on your device, and you can optionally share care with a partner or caregiver through your own private iCloud account — Napling has no accounts and no servers of its own.

Core Features

  • One-tap logging — start a feed, nap, or diaper change from the home screen with a single tap; Napling times it for you
  • Dynamic Island & Lock Screen — an active feeding or nap shows live in the Dynamic Island and on your Lock Screen, so you can check progress without opening the app
  • Widgets — add a Home Screen widget to see the last feed, nap, or diaper change at a glance
  • Siri — log a feed, nap, or diaper change hands-free with Siri while you're holding the baby
  • Shared care — invite a partner or caregiver to co-track your baby through iCloud sharing

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do nap predictions work?

A: Napling looks at your baby's recent sleep patterns and age to suggest when the next nap might be a good idea. These predictions are general guidance only — every baby is different, and Napling's suggestions are not medical advice. Always follow your pediatrician's guidance for your baby's specific needs.

Q: How do I restore my Napling Plus purchase?

A: Open Napling, go to the Plus / upgrade screen, and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used to buy Napling Plus.

Q: Is my baby's data private?

A: Yes. All of your baby's data — feeds, sleep, diapers, growth, milestones, and photos — is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sharing to co-track with a partner or caregiver, that data syncs through your own private iCloud account, which Napling cannot see. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Q: How do I set my baby's birthdate to enable predictions?

A: Go to your baby's profile in Settings and enter their birthdate. Napling uses this, along with recent sleep and feeding history, to power age-appropriate nap and feeding suggestions. Without a birthdate set, these predictions won't appear.

Troubleshooting

A shared entry from my partner isn't showing up

Shared entries sync through iCloud, which can take a few moments. Make sure both devices are signed in to iCloud with an internet connection, and that the caregiver has accepted the sharing invitation.

Widgets aren't updating

Widgets refresh periodically based on iOS's own scheduling. Opening the app briefly will also refresh widget data immediately.
