Privacy Policy for Nestling

Effective July 2026

Quick summary: Nestling has no accounts and no servers of our own. Your baby's data is stored on your device and, if you choose to share care with a partner or caregiver, synced through your own private iCloud account.

What we collect

Nothing. Nestling does not have accounts, advertising, or any servers of our own. We cannot see your baby's feeding logs, sleep sessions, diaper changes, growth measurements, milestones, or photos.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Feeding, sleep, diaper, growth, and milestone entries are stored locally on your iPhone.
  • In your iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, Nestling syncs your data through Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud database — storage that belongs to your Apple account, protected by Apple, and inaccessible to us. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

Sharing care with a partner or caregiver

When you invite a partner, grandparent, or other caregiver to co-track your baby, Apple's CloudKit sharing sends an invitation to the people you choose. Only invited participants can see the shared data. Sharing never passes through any server of ours, and you can stop sharing at any time from the app's share settings.

Photos

When you attach a photo to a milestone, Nestling receives only the photo you explicitly pick — it has no access to the rest of your photo library. Photos are stored and synced the same way as the rest of your data: locally, and through your own private iCloud if enabled.

Growth and health information

Weight, height, and head circumference measurements you enter are treated the same as all other Nestling data — stored locally and, if enabled, synced privately through your own iCloud account. We do not access, analyze, or share this information.

Children's Privacy

Nestling is intended for use by parents and caregivers to track information about their children, not for use by children themselves. We do not knowingly collect any data directly from children, and Nestling has no advertising, messaging, or social features of any kind.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If a future feature ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, with the effective date revised.
